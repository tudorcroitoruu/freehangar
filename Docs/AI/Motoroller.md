# Motoroller — 2026-10-27

Gameplay contains `Motoroller - rideable`, at the original scooter's parking position. The original Motoroller_001 is inactive and unchanged. Reusable prefab: `Assets/Models/motorcycle-old/Optimized/Motoroller_Rideable.prefab`. Editable Blender source: `ArtSource/Motoroller/Motoroller_Rigged.blend`.

Source mesh: 30,000 triangles. Prepared rig: 12,443 triangles (58.5% reduction), with four renderers and original two material/texture sets. The two extra renderers allow separate steering/wheel animation. UVs are retained. Root is at ground level halfway between the axles, meters/unit scale, Unity +Z forward. Geometry is approximately 1.98 m long, 0.86 m wide including mirrors, with 0.247 m wheel radius and 1.298 m wheelbase. Exported FBX is retained; Unity mesh assets bake its axis/scale transforms for clean animation pivots.

Body stays fixed. Handlebars, mirrors, front mudguard, headlamp and fork share an inclined steering pivot. Front wheel is nested below that pivot and spins only on its axle. Rear wheel spins at its own axle. Original assets remain available for comparison.

MotorollerController configures motor speed (12 m/s), acceleration, brake deceleration, wheel radius and wheelbase. It composes the existing BicycleController movement/steering rig, with no crank or fake pedal objects. PlayerBicycleInteraction selects the aimed vehicle and handles the existing camera, walking, instrument, cargo and dismount flow. Engine driving recovers rather than spends stamina; Shift does not boost it. Existing bicycle pedaling/stamina behavior remains available. Wheel spin follows actual distance traveled; wheelbase/radius account for root scale.

Controls: aim and E to mount; W/S forward/reverse; A/D steer; Space brake; mouse look; E to dismount when there is clear supported space. Escape releases the cursor and brakes. `Adjust Riding Position` in the Motoroller Inspector previews the seated camera in Edit Mode. The shared movement rig exposes the steering and wheel references. Rear cargo anchor supports one finished grain bag or the existing BicycleCargoBox interaction; aim and E to secure/retrieve cargo. Grain contents remain owned by GrainContainer.

Collision uses the established upright CharacterController plus a scooter-length box sweep to protect the nose/tail. This is arcade movement, not suspension/balance simulation. Fuel, engine audio, purchases, persistence, and gamepad bindings are not implemented.

Neutral and ±30 degree Unity captures were visually inspected. Scene parking overlap check found no obstacles. Unity compiled without new Console errors.
