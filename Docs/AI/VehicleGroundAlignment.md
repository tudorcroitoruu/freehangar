# Shared vehicle ground alignment

The old shared CharacterController only supported the center of each vehicle and kept its rendered body upright. On inclines this buried one axle and floated the other. BicycleController now initializes VehicleGroundAlignment for every prepared rig (bicycle, Motoroller, Trike, lawnmower and three pickups).

The movement capsule and heading remain upright. At runtime, a child pose carries the model, wheel/steering pivots, cargo, driver view and mower hitch target. Downward probes at the actual wheel positions fit pitch/roll to support, including a two-wheel fallback using contact normals. Tire radii are measured from the authored wheel mesh bounds at initialization. The pose is smoothed and raised to avoid burying tires when contacts differ. Missing front/rear support does not snap the vehicle to an invented plane. Own colliders and the attached mower trailer are excluded from probes. Parked box colliders are copied to the aligned pose, with their upright originals disabled. MotorollerController's body sweep uses the same aligned pose. Bicycle pedal positions use the aligned coordinate frame.

This is terrain-following arcade presentation/collision support, not independent suspension simulation. Highly uneven terrain can still leave a wheel clear of the ground because the vehicle body is rigid. No scene/prefab rewrite or manual wiring is required; the runtime grouping is recreated automatically.

Validation: all seven prepared prefabs aligned on a combined 12-degree longitudinal / 8-degree lateral plane, with tire mesh gaps within 7 mm after supported movement/settling. All three pickups passed 18 real-road traversals; mower/trailer passed the road incline and coast/obstruction suite. Pickup mounting/driving/collision (51), bicycle (22), Motoroller (27), Trike (29), and motor-vehicle coasting checks passed. No new Console errors through cursor 271. A rendered 14-degree uphill pickup view was inspected. Scripts/results: output/Pickups/ValidateGroundAlignment.cs, ground-alignment-validation.txt, SlopeGroundContact.png. No standalone build performed.
