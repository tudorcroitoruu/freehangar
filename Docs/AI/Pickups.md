# Driveable pickups

Gameplay now contains `Pick up - driveable`, `Pick Up Bigger - driveable`, and `Pick Up Last - driveable`. Their prefabs are in `Assets/Models/Pickups/Driveable/`. Original scene vehicles remain inactive, with source meshes, materials, attached wooden/metal beds and LODs preserved. Prepared roots use +Z forward and terrain-level placement; the authored headings and scales are retained. The larger vehicles' `Trailer` children are rigid truck beds, not tow trailers.

The existing BicycleController/MotorollerController and PlayerBicycleInteraction provide E mounting/dismounting, W/S driving/reverse, A/D steering, Space braking and mouse look. PickupController synchronizes both front steering pivots, four axle-centered wheel spins and the steering wheel. Driver views sit inside the left-side cab. No stamina boost. Small pickup: 13 m/s maximum and 3 m/s² acceleration. Larger pickups: 11 m/s and 2.4 m/s². Braking: 8 m/s²; reverse: 30% of forward speed. Dimensions/scale drive clearance and dismount distances. Each bed has four existing-system cargo positions.

Validation: 17 Play Mode targeting/mounting/driving/steering/braking/reverse/wall/ground/dismount checks per pickup (51 total); three lane offsets in both directions across the road incline per pickup (18 successful traversals); seven box-capacity/unloading checks per pickup (21 total). All passed. All three driver-view renders were inspected. No new Console errors after cursor 202. Tests and screenshots: `output/Pickups/`. Scene backup: `output/Pickups/Backups/Gameplay.before-pickups.unity`.

Movement retains the shared upright arcade model, not four-wheel suspension physics. No door opening, fuel, audio, or independent bed/tailgate controls added.

Steering-wheel correction: imported pickup wheel meshes lie in local YZ, so local X is their column axis. PickupController now applies the steering angle around local X after the authored rest rotation (previously local Y, which tilted the wheel). All three pickups passed 27 axis/hub/left/right/centering assertions; full-right driver-view render visually checked. Validation: output/Pickups/ValidateSteeringColumn.cs.

Exit inertia: motor-driven BicycleController rigs preserve speed on dismount and coast autonomously through the same terrain/body collision and wheel-animation path. `exitCoastDuration` defaults to 0.8 seconds (maximum stop time; brakingDeceleration can stop sooner); set zero for an immediate stop. Steering smoothly centers. Parked/cargo collider states are restored when stopped; remounting during the coast preserves momentum and the original collider snapshot. Forward/reverse decay, pause, wall blocking, stationary exit, remount and all three pickup player/camera dismount flows passed. Lawn mower trailer towing during coast and existing bicycle regression passed. Validation: output/Pickups/ValidateExitCoast.cs, ValidateExitInteraction.cs; output/Lawnmower/ValidateMowerCoast.cs.

Steering direction and camera return: pickup steering-wheel spin now uses negative local X relative to steering input. Shared PlayerBicycleInteraction returns yaw/pitch smoothly to the configured driver view after 3 seconds without mouse-look input. Any new mouse movement cancels the return and restarts the delay; paused time and disabled input do not advance it. Mounting resets the timer and smoothing state. Inspector tuning: cameraReturnDelay (3 seconds), cameraReturnSmoothTime (0.6 seconds). Tests: all three pickups passed camera delay/no-snap/interruption/return checks plus normal vehicle interactions; steering-column tests now verify the reversed rotation direction.

Pickup road-camera motion: PickupController generates distance-based smoothed noise, weighted by actual travel speed and capped changes in vertical speed. Default intensity 0.65 keeps translation under 3.9 mm vertically / 1.3 mm laterally and angular movement below 0.09 degrees. No FOV changes or accumulated yaw drift. Constant slopes do not continuously amplify bumps. Stationary motion decays to zero; pause freezes it; mounting/dismounting resets it. PlayerBicycleInteraction applies the offset additively to the existing look/recenter view only for pickups. Tune `roadCameraIntensity` (0 disables) in PickupController. This reacts to actual vehicle movement over terrain/collision geometry, not decorative non-colliding rock meshes. Play Mode checks passed for all three pickups: flat/uneven/reverse motion, limits, stopping, pause, disable/reset, and the existing full camera-return/vehicle interaction tests. Tests: output/Pickups/ValidateRoadCamera.cs.

Bump roll adjustment: increased the subtle Z rocking with an independently tunable `roadCameraRollDegrees` (default 0.45° before intensity, at most 0.293° with default intensity). Slower distance-based noise and 0.18-second smoothing create gentle side-to-side rocking, strongest on bumps. Tests confirm roll in both directions, bounded amplitude, stop/pause/reset behavior on all three pickups.
