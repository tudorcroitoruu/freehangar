# Bicycle implementation, 2026-10-26

Gameplay now contains `Bicycle - red rideable`. The original `bicycle` scene object is inactive and its source FBX is unchanged. Reusable prefab: `Assets/Models/bicycle/Optimized/Bicycle_Rideable.prefab`. Editable source: `ArtSource/Bicycle/Bicycle_Optimized.blend`; authoring scripts and checks: `output/Bicycle`.

The 49,587-triangle source became 8,064 imported triangles (83.7% reduction), plus a 12-triangle rear rack. Source UVs and surface textures are retained. `Bicycle_Red_BaseColor.png` is a new red-paint albedo derived from the original frame map. The frame, steering assembly, front/rear wheels, crank and left/right pedals are separate meshes. Root origin is centered on the bike, Unity +Z points toward the front wheel, +Y is up. Moving parts use local animation pivots. Realistic bicycle scale is approximately 1.8m long with 0.34m wheel radius.

Approach and aim at the bike: E mounts. W/S pedal forward/reverse, A/D steer, Space brakes, mouse looks around, E dismounts after a clearance/ground check. Escape releases the cursor and applies braking. The existing player camera is temporarily attached to the rider view; walking, bag input and held instrument are suspended and restored. Movement is an upright CharacterController arcade model, not a wheel-physics/balance simulation. No persistence or controller/gamepad bindings were added.

Rear rack has two cargo slots. Carry a finished filled bag, aim at the bike and E secures it. Aim at the bag and use the existing E interaction to retrieve it. Bag mass and contents stay in GrainContainer; taking it off the rack restores independent drop ownership. The placed sample `Bicycle cargo box` can be secured with E when beside the bike, then unloaded with E at a clear nearby ground position. Other boxes need BicycleCargoBox and a collider/renderer. Cargo is kinematic while secured; its colliders are temporarily disabled during riding to prevent self-collision. Purchasing and generic box carrying remain outside this mini controller.

PlayerController calls PlayerBicycleInteraction before normal E bag handling; it calls ResetLook after dismount. BagInteraction.TryStow is the explicit carried-bag transfer point. To reuse in another scene, add PlayerBicycleInteraction to the existing PlayerController and assign player, bicycle and held instrument; the prefab carries the bike-side references.

Validation: Unity compilation passed. 17 riding/mount/camera/braking/box checks and 10 filled-bag checks passed in Play Mode. An isolated wall/floor test reached the wall without crossing it and retained ground support. Unity scene preview inspected. Console after the final feature imports had no new errors; older unrelated imported URP WaterFall shader errors remain in history. No standalone build or hardware performance benchmark was run.

2026-10-26 riding controls follow-up: steering uses SmoothDampAngle (0.14s default) for both the steering mesh/front wheel and the movement heading. Maximum steer is configurable. BicycleController Inspector now has Adjust Riding Position, opening an Undo-aware window with camera offset/pitch, seat-anchor movement, a seated Scene view preview, restore-view and Save scene. Runtime riders can press V or release the cursor with Escape and click Adjust riding position. Sliders preview sideways/height/forward offset and pitch; Save & ride stores a local per-bike PlayerPrefs view, Cancel restores the previous view. The adjustment panel brakes the bike and prevents mouse clicks from recapturing the cursor. Walking view is restored on dismount.

Holding either Shift while pedaling forward increases the speed target from 7 to 10.5m/s (configurable 1.5 multiplier), consuming 12 stamina/second via the existing StaminaManager and HUD. No stamina is consumed for normal pedaling, stopped/braking, or view adjustment. Exhaustion disables boost; normal speed resumes and the existing 2s delay/15-per-second recovery applies. A held-Shift exhaustion latch waits for 20% stamina before allowing boost again, preventing frame-to-frame toggling. Release Shift to resume normal pedaling. StaminaManager.Tick gained an optional per-activity rate; existing bag-work and shovel costs are preserved.

Validation: 24 isolated Play Mode checks passed for steering easing/reversal/centering, faster speed and stamina drain/exhaustion/recovery, camera live preview, free-cursor ownership, cancel, saved-preference reload, bounds and dismount restoration. Six Editor checks passed for custom Inspector, Undo/Redo, seated preview orbit configuration and Scene view restoration.
